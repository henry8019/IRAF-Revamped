# Community conduct

People working with IRAF bring different levels of astronomy and software experience. Ask questions freely, give credit for contributions, and keep reviews focused on the observation, calculation, or code. When results differ, share the inputs and reasoning that would let someone else reproduce them.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and sharing someone’s private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Use the reporting menu on the relevant GitHub comment or post to flag abuse. Please avoid reposting personal information in a public issue.
